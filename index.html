    INTERVIEWER MODE

    Explain your decisions, not only your tools.

    Use this rubric after completing the worksheet. Be explicit about what the evidence proves, what remains a hypothesis, and how you verify containment.

    Suggested interview walkthrough

    1. State the incident hypothesis and initial priority. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
    2. Walk through the timeline and explain how you correlated records.
    3. Identify the highest-risk business impact and the first containment decision.
    4. Explain the trade-off, decision authority, and rollback condition.
    5. Show a resource-level verification test, not merely a console status.
    6. Describe the evidence gap, residual risk, and one detection or control improvement.
